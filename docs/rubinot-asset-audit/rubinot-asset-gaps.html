Sealed in the .rtc packs

690 references

RubinOT has no data/images directory on disk. Its image tree is inside data/dat-01.rtc, dat-02.rtc and dat-03.rtc — 141 MB across 2,598 entries.

RTC v3 header — 32 bytes plaintext, everything after is ciphertext 0x00 char[4] magic "RTC\0" 0x04 u32 version 3 0x08 u64 entryCount 5 / 9 / 2584 0x10 u64 indexOffset == fileSize − indexSize (index sits at EOF) 0x18 u64 indexSize == entryCount × 32

Not extractable from what's in this folder

Shannon entropy of the payload measures 8.0000 bits/byte over 4 MB samples in all three files, and the 82,688-byte index is encrypted too — 0 of 2,584 candidate u64 offsets fall in bounds, and monotonicity is exactly 50%. No filenames, offsets or sizes survive in plaintext. Not a repeating-XOR keystream (period match rate 1/256 at every stride tested), and no zstd, lz4, gzip or PNG magic anywhere in 141 MB.

The RubinOT executable isn't in the tree, so the key material is unavailable — the mount and decrypt are entirely C++-side. CrystalOTC can't read them either: it has no custom PHYSFS_Archiver, its own crypto is XChaCha20-Poly1305 (and compiled off), and its packs are plain ZIPs from build_pak.py.
